## Starting observations

These came from one engineer's job search, in one market. They are offered as
a starting point, not as rules. Keep the ones that hold for this user and
delete the rest.

- **Apply on a partial match.** A long requirements list usually describes an
  ideal candidate, not a cut-off.
- **Years of experience is a harder gate than the rest.** Recruiters flex most
  requirements but screen strictly on stated years, and may not count
  non-professional work toward them.
- **Never fill a gap with an invention.** Gaps shape honest positioning and
  interview prep. Invented titles, degrees, or keywords come out in the
  interview.
- **Every public claim must pass three tests.** It is defensible at the depth
  it implies, it is relevant to the reader, and it attracts work the user
  actually wants.
