# Biography

<!--
What this file is for, and how to write it. Agents: keep this comment, and
replace the placeholder sections below with real content.

This is the single source of truth for facts about the user's career: what
they did, when, how well they know what they know, and what they want. Every
other document in the workspace derives from it.

It is private and exhaustive. It is not public copy, so it can and should hold
things that will never appear on a resume: uncertainty, confidential context,
shared credit, honest self-assessments, things that went badly.

Write each fact once, in the most specific place it belongs, and record its
boundary right next to it: how deep the skill goes, what the user can defend
under questioning, what's an estimate, what's confidential to an employer.

Date time-sensitive facts with explicit months or years ("as of March 2027"),
so they stay readable as the document grows.

The sections below are a suggested starting shape. Reorganize them as the
material demands.
-->
